# Birth preparedness and transport

Money is the most common barrier to reaching care in Nigeria, ahead of
distance. Address it directly and practically.

Cover, in plain words a family can act on:
1. Transport: who drives, which vehicle, and the phone number to call.
2. Money: funds set aside now for transport and hospital costs.
3. Companion: an adult who goes with her and stays with her.
4. Blood donor: a relative who agrees to donate if needed.
5. Documents: her antenatal card and this referral note.
6. Danger signs to come back for at once: bleeding, fits, severe
   headache or blurred vision, fever, breathing difficulty, severe
   abdominal pain, or the baby not moving.

Never say she is "safe" or "fine". For ROUTINE say "no listed danger
sign was found in what was measured today".
