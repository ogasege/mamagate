## Narration

**1. Problem (0:00-0:20)**
In Nigeria, fewer than half of births have a skilled attendant. When a
pregnant woman shows a danger sign, the community health worker beside her
must decide fast, then get her to hospital. In one Kano study, only one in
ten women given pre-referral treatment completed the referral. MamaGate is
built to close that gap.

**2. The gate (0:20-0:45)**
A synthetic case: thirty-four weeks pregnant, blood pressure one sixty-eight
over one twelve, severe headache, protein in the urine. Before any AI runs,
a rules gate in plain code checks thirty-five danger-sign rules from
Nigeria's Standing Orders and WHO charts. Refer now, in about a millisecond,
with zero AI tokens, and the source page for every rule.

**3. The handoff (0:45-1:15)**
Now a Strands agent on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore completes the referral. It
picks the hospital with a real road time from Amazon Location Service,
writes a structured referral note, and code checks it before anything is
sent. A steering handler enforces the order. It pre-alerts the hospital
through Amazon SNS and schedules an arrival check with EventBridge
Scheduler.

**4. The family card (1:15-1:45)**
The family gets a card in plain words: where to go, what to bring, and when
to rush. It is fixed wording, never generated by the AI, in English,
Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo, and it can be read aloud.

**5. Closing the loop (1:45-2:00)**
At the hospital, the referral arrives before the woman does. One tap marks
her arrived, cancels the supervisor alert, and tells the clinic. If she
never arrives, the supervisor is alerted.

**6. Safety (2:00-2:15)**
If a worker asks for a drug dose, MamaGate refuses in code, before any
model is called, and points to the right page of the Standing Orders.
Urgency can be raised, never lowered.

**7. Proof (2:15-2:35)**
On synthetic cases, the gate caught all twenty-four refer-now cases and
escalated none of the routine ones. On four hundred and fifty-two real
antenatal readings, the data showed a blood-pressure follow-up gap, so I
added a WHO aligned test-now action and measured the change.

**8. Limits and learning (2:35-2:50)**
The rules are not yet clinically reviewed, and the data is synthetic. What
I learned: let code decide what must never be wrong, and let the agent
coordinate around it. The code is open source.
